|07|09__[ |15About Display Name/Nick Menu|07: |09]___________________________|07

|07 You may view or edit your display name any time with the
 |15/|11nick |07command. The name color, prefix, and suffix strings
 are all independently modifiable, or all may be randomized
 for you. A preview is shown so you can see how it will look
 while in chat.

|07 Chat is paused while in this menu. Any messages received
 while in this menu get shown upon returning to chat, so you
 won't miss anything. If |15/|11sound |07is turned on, you will still
 hear mention beeps while in this menu.
 
|07 Your configured display name persists across multiple chat
 sessions until reset by the sysop.
