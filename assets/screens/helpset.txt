|07|09__[ |15About Chatter Settings|07: |09]_________________________________|07

|07 You may view or edit your settings any time with the |15/|11set
|07 command, letting you modify things like your displayed username
 (aka nick), default room, default text color, chat sounds, join
 message, exit message, and theme.
 
 Some settings can be accessed more directly:
   |15/|11nick |08.... |07Edit display name/nick
   |15/|11sound |08... |07Toggle chat sounds
   |15/|11theme |08... |07Change theme

|07 Chat is paused while viewing settings. Any messages received
 while in this menu get shown upon returning to chat, so you 
 won't miss anything. If |15/|11sound |07is turned on, you will still
 hear mention beeps while in this menu.
 
|07 Settings persist across multiple chat sessions until reset by
 the sysop.
