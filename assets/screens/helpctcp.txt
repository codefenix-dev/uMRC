|09__[ |15How to use CTCP commands|07: |09]_______________________________|07

 CTCP (Client-to-Client Protocol) commands are special messages
 that can be sent to a channel or other clients.

 Usage:
   |15/|11ctcp |15target |09command|07

 A |15target |07is a user or room name. |15* |07targets all.

 Supported commands:
   |09VERSION |08......... |07Remote client info and version #
   |09TIME |08............ |07Local time of remote client's BBS
   |09PING |10<string> |08... |07Returns string received by the client
   |09CLIENTINFO |08...... |07Remote client's supported CTCP commands
