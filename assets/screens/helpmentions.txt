|07 Every time someone mentions you by name or sends you a direct
 message in chat, the message is highlighted onscreen and stored
 to a buffer, the mention counter goes up by 1, and the terminal
 beeps (if sounds are on).

|07 You can view the mention buffer by typing |15/|11mentions |07or by 
 simply pressing the |15UP |07hotkey. This also resets the mention 
 counter.

|07 Chat is paused while viewing the buffer. Any messages received 
 while viewing buffer get shown upon returning to chat. If chat 
 sounds are turned on, you will still hear mention beeps while 
 viewing the buffer.

|07 The mention buffer persists until you exit chat.
