|07 Every time someone |15mentions |07you by name or sends you a direct
 message in chat, the message is highlighted on screen and
 stored to a buffer, the mention counter goes up by 1, and the
 terminal beeps (if |15/|11sound |07is turned on).

|07 You can view the mention buffer by typing |15/|11mentions |07or by
 simply pressing the |15UP |07hotkey. This also resets the mention
 counter.

|07 Chat is paused while viewing the buffer. Any messages received
 while viewing the buffer get shown upon returning to chat, so
 you won't miss anything. If |15/|11sound |07is turned on, you will still
 hear mention beeps while viewing the buffer.

|07 The mention buffer persists until you exit chat.
