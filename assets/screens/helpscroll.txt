|07|09__[ |15About the Scrollback|07: |09]___________________________________|07

|07 uMRC maintains its own scrollback buffer of all messages 
 shown since entering chat.

|07 You can view the scrollback buffer by typing |15/|11scroll |07or by
 simply pressing the |15PGUP |07hotkey.

|07 Chat is paused while viewing the buffer. Any messages received
 while viewing the buffer get shown upon returning to chat, so
 you won't miss anything. If |15/|11sound |07is turned on, you will still
 hear mention beeps while viewing the buffer.
 
|07 The scrollback buffer persists until you exit chat.
